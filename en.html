The problem

If you only check the caption, you miss most of the delivery.

Most tools read text and metrics. The brand mention, the coupon and the offer often live in the audio and on screen, in feed reels and in stories, which vanish the next day.

What the caption shows

"Obsessed with this new kit, hurry before it sells out!"

  • No brand named
  • No coupon or link
  • No #ad
  • Story: gone everywhere after 24 h

What DutraTrace finds

  • SPEECHBrand, discount and "link in my bio" said out loud
  • SCREENCoupon code, product and brand @ tagged on the image
  • CAPTIONNo #ad or "paid partnership" up front
  • ARCHIVEStory saved with date, time and SHA-256 hash, even after it disappears

FAQ

Before you ask

What data do you collect?

Only content the creator published publicly: posts, stories, videos and captions. We don't collect comments, follower data or anything from private profiles.

Does the creator know they're being monitored?

No. Capture doesn't interact with the profile: it doesn't follow, like, comment or show up in story views.

Do you tell us whether a rule was broken?

We flag points of attention with the quote and the file so your team can decide. Legal conclusions stay with you or your lawyer.

I don't want to share my creators. Can I still test it?

Yes. Tell us your niche and we'll run the test report on public profiles from that niche, so you can see the output without sharing anything.

Which networks and languages do you cover?

Instagram (feed and stories), TikTok and YouTube; other networks on request. Speech and on-screen text are read in English, Spanish, Portuguese and other major languages.
